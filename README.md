## 👋 About me

I'm a Data Scientist with 4.5 years in customer analytics at a leading retailer (26M+ customers), and about 20 years of liking computers more than is reasonable. I take a business problem end to end: find the opportunity, build the model, say who to target and with which offer, then measure the incremental effect and come back with a plan for the next launch.

What I'm proudest of: pipelines that kept running through unstable storage, clusters, resources and tight deadlines. They failed, they recovered, and the launches still landed on time.

I started with HTML in a Notepad window, moved on to Flash, C# and a lot of university maths, and ended up in ML. The wonder has turned into a craft, but a model that finally improves and a pipeline that finishes cleanly on millions of rows still make me happy.

Based in Batumi 🇬🇪 · Remote · Russian (native), English (professional)
